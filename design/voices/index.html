How these were made

Qwen3-TTS 1.7B VoiceDesign (int8) via Wan2GP on the RTX 4060. Each description was rendered three times (seeds 101/202/303). The best take by pitch fit, pace and a Whisper check is saved as a 24 kHz mono reference wav. The test lines are cloned from that wav with Qwen3-TTS Base, then trimmed and saved as mono 64 kbps MP3 at −16 LUFS. One short line takes about 2–3 s to generate once the model is loaded. Direct voice design and ICL cloning are kept under “other modes” to show why they aren't used.
