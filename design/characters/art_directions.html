Buhísimo: art direction test

Four styles, three characters, two tries each (Z-Image Turbo on your laptop). Pick the style (or mix) that feels right; we then design each character properly in it. The first test (the commercial-looking capybaras and my SVG) is on compare.html.

Chiva folk art

Colombian hand-painted bus style: bold outlines, bright flowers. The most Colombian and distinctive. Outlines make printable line art easy.

Mid-century modern

Simple geometric shapes, 4 colours, no outlines. Calm and clean, the least distracting.

Cut-paper collage

Layered paper with texture and Wayuu patterns. Charming and crafty, but hard to turn into printable line art.

Cartoon series

Upright characters wearing clothes. Expressive, but closer to generic cartoons.
